The challenge

Infrastructure incidents do not stay isolated.

Cyber events, physical anomalies and operational disruptions can create consequences across connected assets and functions. Operators need a defensible view of what may be exposed, what is actually affected and what response is justified.

Multi-domain signals

Cyber, physical and operational information often arrives through separate systems and teams.

Technical dependencies

One incident can affect downstream assets, functions and services through complex infrastructure relationships.

Operational decisions

Response needs to be evidence-based, understandable and compatible with human operational authority.

The concept

From fragmented events to operator-oriented situational awareness.

Bastion Grid combines event correlation, asset mapping, dependency awareness, cascading-exposure assessment, operational impact assessment and controlled response support.
